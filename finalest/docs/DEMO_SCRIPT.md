# FININTEL — 3 minute judge demo

1. **Front Page** — “We designed FININTEL like a financial newspaper, not a normal stock dashboard. Light mode looks editorial; Dark mode looks like a modern terminal.”
2. Select **RELIANCE**. Point to **Market Pulse** and the three independent dimensions: momentum, volume and sentiment.
3. Enter a portfolio and choose **Moderate** risk. Click **Analyse Stock with All Agents**.
4. Point to the five front-page agent cards, then open **AI Desk** to show all six specialists and their structured `score + confidence + reasoning` contract.
5. Open **Investigations**. Show the weighted contributions, full reasoning trace and Safety / Uncertainty layer.
6. Open **Filings**. Say: “The Fundamental Agent retrieves source text first. If the filing is missing, it abstains instead of hallucinating.”
7. Open **Portfolio**. Show that the same stock data produces different risk/final outputs for Conservative, Moderate and Aggressive profiles.
8. In the sidebar turn on **Simulate conflicting signals** or **missing market feed**, rerun, and show the reduced confidence instead of a crash.
9. Open **Performance**. Show latency, confidence and concentration saved in the persistent session log.
10. Close with: **“FININTEL is an auditable multi-agent research pipeline, not a black-box stock tip.”**
